LIBERATION FLEET COMMUNITY STANDARDS
(Acceptable Use Policy)

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Liberation Fleet is for adults (18+) coordinating mutual aid through crews, fleets, gift logging, Library of Things sharing, and related community tools. By using the Service you agree to these standards together with the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. AGE
You must be at least 18 years old. Accounts for or depicting minors in a sexual context are forbidden.

2. ILLEGAL CONTENT — ZERO TOLERANCE
You may not create, share, request, or store:
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, grooming, or online enticement of a minor
- Child sex trafficking content
- Non-consensual intimate images (including deepfakes or AI generated content of a real person)
- Credible threats of violence or content that facilitates violent crime
- Other material that is illegal under United States federal law

Reports of child sexual exploitation are treated as highest priority. When you report such content, a temporary plaintext evidence snapshot may be retained so we can file required reports to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

3. HARASSMENT AND ABUSE
Do not stalk, harass, dox, or target other users. Use Block and Report. Crew or fleet kick proposals are for membership governance; they do not replace platform reports for illegal content. Roles such as Organizer or Moderator do not authorize harassment or illegal conduct.

4. SPAM AND SCAMS
Do not use the Service for unsolicited spam, phishing, or financial fraud. Do not misrepresent gift amounts, donation activity, emergency needs, verification status, identity, or affiliation to deceive crewmates or fleet members.

5. MUTUAL AID HONESTY
Record gifts and emergency requests in good faith. Do not invent gifts, inflate amounts, fake middleman completions, or abuse season, threshold, cycle, or priority mechanics to take unfair advantage of others.

6. LIBRARY OF THINGS
The Library of Things is community resource sharing, not a marketplace run by Liberation Fleet.
- Describe offerings and condition accurately.
- Do not file false broken, lost, or maintenance reports.
- Do not abuse request ranking, task boards, or contribution credit.
- Digital goods must be legal for you to share; do not upload malware, stolen content, or illegal material.
- Respect return windows, care expectations, and crew/fleet rules for borrowed durables.

7. ENCRYPTION AND REPORTS
Most crew, fleet, chat, forum, proposal, rules, DM, library payload/task, gift-display, and media content is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read it in normal operation. If you Report content, your device may send a one-time decrypted snapshot of that item (and limited metadata) sealed for safety review. Routine chat is not monitored. Knowingly false reports intended to harm others are prohibited.

8. ATTACHMENTS AND ADULT CONTENT
Attach only allowed media types. Do not attempt to upload executable, script, or HTML/SVG payloads. Prefer the default “encrypt attached files” setting for sensitive material. Honor adult-content labels and gates; 18+ material must still comply with these standards and the law.

9. VOICE AND SOCIAL SPACES
Voice rooms, text chats, forums, and direct messages are shared community spaces. Do not disrupt rooms, evade blocks, or use anonymous/advocate modes (where available) to harass others.

10. ENFORCEMENT
We may freeze accounts (including pending a safety review), remove or quarantine content identifiers, preserve report evidence, and report apparent child sexual exploitation to NCMEC when we have actual knowledge. We may refuse service to anyone who violates these standards or the Terms of Use.

11. CONTACT
Use in-app Report for abusive or illegal content. For account issues, contact the operators through the channel published on our website when available.

Operators: engineering and ops documentation for report packets, NCMEC filing, and vendor triage lives in the project docs folder (SAFETY-REPORTING.md). That documentation is for operators; it does not change your rights under this Acceptable Use Policy.

These standards are part of your agreement to use Liberation Fleet together with the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
